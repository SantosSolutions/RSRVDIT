About

With over a decade of experience spanning sales, banking, and IT — I've built a career around service. Today I'm a Senior Helpdesk Specialist at a managed services provider, supporting Medical, Financial, and Government clients onsite, remote, and hybrid. My day-to-day runs the full stack of modern IT support: Microsoft 365 and Exchange Online administration, Entra ID and Active Directory identity management, Azure Virtual Desktop environments, and Cisco Meraki / Ubiquiti UniFi networking — including solo on-site deployments where I stand up firewalls, switching, Wi-Fi, and monitoring end to end. I run point on Huntress EDR security incidents from triage through remediation, and I led my team's adoption of AI-assisted workflows — earning an AI Champion award. As a bilingual (English/Spanish) professional, I communicate across every level — from C-suite to end users. Off the clock, I'm studying for the CCNA, running a Raspberry Pi home lab, and serving at church — helping with production, YouTube livestreams, and building sermon notes through the YouVersion Bible App. I move with faith, intention, and persistence — always building toward what's next.
